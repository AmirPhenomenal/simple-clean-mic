# Roadmap

Where Clean Mic is heading. Each item links to an issue with details; comment there to share
ideas or say you'd like to work on it. New ideas are welcome in
[Discussions](https://github.com/AmirPhenomenal/simple-clean-mic/discussions) or as a
[feature request](https://github.com/AmirPhenomenal/simple-clean-mic/issues/new/choose).

Everything stays **free, local and private**: no cloud, no account.

## Big features

| Feature | What it means | Issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VST / CLAP plugins** | Add your own audio plugins (de-esser, reverb, channel strip...) to the chain. | [#9](https://github.com/AmirPhenomenal/simple-clean-mic/issues/9) |
| **Record mode** | Record your voice to WAV / FLAC / MP3 / Opus with a timed transcript (SRT / VTT), optionally embedded in the file. For video editing, podcasts, notes. | [#10](https://github.com/AmirPhenomenal/simple-clean-mic/issues/10) |
| **Live transcript** | Turn it on and everything you say is appended to a text file with timestamps. | [#11](https://github.com/AmirPhenomenal/simple-clean-mic/issues/11) |

Record mode and live transcript share one offline speech-to-text engine
([whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggerganov/whisper.cpp)).
